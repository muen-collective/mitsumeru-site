ASCII Water Reveal

A height-field wave simulation on a coarse grid. Every cell above a small threshold draws one ASCII glyph, so a moving pointer stirs the surface and the ripples read as trails of characters.

The physics is the classic string equation: each cell moves toward the mean of its four neighbours, with damping. The glyph picks its brightness from that height.

Move or press the pointer over the canvas. Faster movement makes bigger, brighter ripples.

Drop-in usage: createAsciiWaterReveal(el, { settings }) — it returns a handle with stop(), a live settings object, and stir() / drop() for driving it from code.
