Mission

Mitsumeru exists because the distance between imagining and making has always been too long.

You see it. You can't build it yet. So it dies in a Figma file, or a note, or a conversation that ends with "someday."

AI shortened the distance. But it didn't give you a place to work. Just tools.

Mitsumeru is the place. A harness for the loop: imagine, build, craft. Repeat until it holds.

01 — Imagine

You don't start with a brief. You start with a disturbance — a feeling that something is wrong, or missing, or not yet possible.

Bring that. Mitsumeru gives it a face. Then you look at it, and you decide if it's worth your life.

Mitsumeru gives you a workbench. Generate, break, regenerate, wire it up, tear it down. The AI doesn't decide when you're finished. You do.

Why Mitsumeru

BYOK out of the box

Bring your own API keys. No vendor lock-in. Works with the providers you already pay for.

Model and process agnostic

Switch between image gen, LLMs, and custom pipelines without changing tools.

Built around process, not features

Other harnesses list capabilities. Mitsumeru maps to the design workflow: Visualize, Build, Polish. Plugins, skills, and prompts built by Muen to support that loop.

RunningHub integration

Paste a workflow URL and get a working UI panel. No config files. No boilerplate.

Built on DeepSeek Harness

Chosen deliberately, for one reason: its plugin architecture is open. Because the core accepts new skills, panels and workflows, Muen could build a harness for designers instead of dressing up a model. The core is theirs. Everything you touch is ours.

Muen designs the skills, plugins, and system prompts that make Mitsumeru work. Not a feature list — a process. The harness exists because the agency needed it to exist.
