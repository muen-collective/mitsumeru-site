Quick Scan Button

A bordered button rebuilt from scanbutton.framer.website, measured off that page at 1728px. The label sits in a 19px well that clips, with a second copy stacked under it; hovering slides the pair up one line.

Four 11px marks sit centred on the corners, so 5px of each hangs outside. Their two strokes cross at the mark's own centre, which puts the crossing point on the box corner — a `+`, not a bracket. The corner strokes and the scan rule carry the accent; the box rules are white at opacity .1, which is what reads as red crosses joined by a grey line. All CSS and one element tree, no canvas.
